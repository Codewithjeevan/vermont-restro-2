Mess Module Plan

A separate "Mess" section in the admin panel for monthly tiffin members: daily menu, who got food and who did not, extras, monthly money, and ingredient stock that moves every day. POS stays untouched except one "Mess Collection" card on the register report.

01 Aaj ka tareeka: the Excel sheet

What the sheet tells us about the business

  • Rolling cycles, not calendar months. A member starts any day and runs 30 or 31 days. Renewal is a new row.
  • Three meal slots exist (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) but most members take Dinner only. Some take different quantities per slot ("Lunch + 2 Dinner").
  • Quantity is persons/tiffins, and the price is negotiated per member, not a strict rate × persons (2 persons dinner = 500 or 550, driver = 130, 5 days = 57).
  • Diet is Veg / Non-veg / Both / "no beef", and it decides which menu the member gets.
  • Pickup vs Delivery matters for the daily list and for phone and address.
  • Money comes in pieces: advance, part payments, card + cash splits, and "paid" written in the notes column.
  • Extras live in Notes ("2 cool drinks 1 water = 7 pending") and get lost.

What the sheet cannot do

  • No record of which day a member took or missed a meal. "Sheet1" was an attempt at this (NAME × LUNCH × DINNER) and stayed empty.
  • No link to the kitchen: no daily menu, no headcount for cooking, no ingredient stock movement.
  • No reminders for expiring members, no dues list, no cash/card total for the register.
  • Old cycles get overwritten; there is no history per member.

03 How it works: two loops

The module runs on two loops. The monthly loop is about people and money. The daily loop is about food and stock. They meet on the Meal Register, where "who is expected today" comes from subscriptions and "who was served" feeds both attendance and cooking numbers.

Monthly loop people and money

  1. Create the member once (name, phone, building, diet, pickup or delivery).
  2. Start a subscription: plan, meals and persons per slot, start date, agreed price. End date fills automatically.
  3. Collect money whenever it comes: advance, part, rest. Each payment is cash, card or bank and lands on the open register.
  4. Three days before the end date the member appears in "Expiring". One tap on Renew makes the next cycle.
  5. Balance and history stay on the member ledger forever.

Daily loop food and stock

  1. Set today's menu per slot and diet (or copy).
  2. The daily sheet is generated: expected headcount, production plan, ingredient needs vs stock.
  3. Kitchen cooks. Counter or delivery marks each member Served / Not taken as tiffins go out.
  4. Extras are added when someone asks for something more.
  5. Day close per slot: confirm cooked portions and leftover. Consumption posts to stock, leftover posts to waste.

A day in the mess, with this module

  1. 08:30 MenuManager opens Daily Menu, copies yesterday's dinner, swaps Dal for Rajma. Portion per person stays 1.

05 Screens, one by one

Each screen below has a mock, the rules it enforces, and what it writes to the database. Names and numbers in the mocks come from the client's own sheet.

5.1 Plans (packages)

A plan is a price list entry, not a contract. It gives the default price per person per cycle and which meals are included. Staff can still type an agreed price on the subscription, which is how the sheet works today (driver at 130, Arif/Khaled at 750).

5.2 Members and subscriptions

A member is created once. A subscription is one cycle for that member. Renewing copies the last subscription with new dates, so the monthly re-typing disappears.

Subscription rules

  • Status is automatic: Active while today is inside the dates, Expiring when the end date is within the alert window (default 3 days), Expired after the end date. Paused and Closed are set by staff.
  • Pause (member travelling): enter from and to dates; the end date moves forward by the same number of days. Attendance rows are not generated for paused days.
  • Pro-rata: if the cycle is shorter than the plan's cycle, the suggested price = per-meal rate × meals × days. Staff can still override.
  • Renew: creates the next subscription starting the day after the old end date, same meals, same price. Unpaid balance carries on the ledger, not on the new subscription.
  • Diet drives the menu: a Veg member is counted against the Veg menu; "Both" counts against Non-veg by default and can be flipped per day from the register.

5.3 Daily menu planner

Dishes are ordinary Food Menus, so the recipe is entered once in the existing Food Menu screen and reused every day. The planner only picks which dishes go to which slot and diet, and how many portions each person gets.

  • Mess dishes sit in a "Mess Kitchen" food category. A flag hides them from the POS grid if the client does not sell them à la carte.
  • A dish without a recipe shows an amber warning on the planner; it still works for attendance, only stock is skipped for that dish.
  • Weekly template: 7 days × slot × diet, saved once, loaded in one tap, then edited for the day.

5.4 Meal register (attendance)

This is the screen the counter keeps open during the slot. It answers "kitne ko khana gaya, kitne ko nahi gaya" for breakfast, lunch and dinner separately.

Three ways to mark

  • Live at the counter: tap Served as the tiffin is handed over. Best for pickup.
  • Delivery batch: filter Delivery, print the route list, mark on return or when the driver calls.
  • Paper first: print the tick sheet, tick by hand, enter the exceptions in one minute using the bulk buttons.

Skips in advance: if a member says "kal nahi aaunga", staff enter a skip for that date and slot from the member page. The daily sheet then reduces the headcount, and the planner cooks less. Whether a skip earns a credit is a setting (see decisions).

5.5 Extras: "aaj mujhe ye chahiye"

An extra is a one-off line for a member on a date and slot. It can be any Food Menu item (so price and recipe come for free) or a custom line ("1 small water"). It appears on the daily sheet so the kitchen prepares it, and it is settled in one of two ways.

  • Advance requests ("kal biryani chahiye, 2 extra") are extras with tomorrow's date. The planner adds them to planned portions.
  • An extra for a dish with a recipe consumes stock at day close like any other portion.
  • Pay-now extras create a Mess payment immediately, so they reach the register card the same night.

5.6 Daily sheet: how it is generated

The sheet is not typed. It is built from three sources for a date: active subscriptions (who and how many), the menu planner (what), and extras (what more). It is regenerated on every open, so a new member added at noon appears at dinner.

Generation rules

  1. For the chosen date, find every subscription whose start–end range covers it and which is not paused that day.
  2. For each of its meal rows (slot, persons), create an expected attendance row if it does not exist yet, status Pending. Rows already marked are never overwritten.
  3. Apply advance skips: matching rows become Skipped and drop out of the headcount.
  4. Pull the planned menu for each slot and diet. If a slot has expected members but no menu, show a red "menu missing" line.
  5. Planned portions per dish = Σ (expected persons × portion per person) + extras for that dish, plus any manual buffer.
  6. Ingredient need per dish = planned portions × recipe consumption. Compare with current stock from the existing stock query; anything short is highlighted.
  7. Attach the extras list and the delivery route list.

5.7 Day close and inventory

Mess food is cooked in bulk, so stock moves on cooked portions, not on each tiffin. Day close is where the kitchen confirms what was actually cooked and what was left, and the system posts the ingredient movement.

  • Waste uses the existing Waste data (tbl_wastes supports a food menu and quantity), created automatically from the leftover column.
  • Variance (planned vs cooked vs served) is stored per dish so the Consumption and Variance report can show where food is over-cooked.
  • Dish without recipe: consumption is skipped and the row is flagged, so the manager can add the recipe later and re-post.
  • Reopen is Admin-only and reverses the posted rows before re-posting.

5.8 Payments and member ledger

Money is charged once per cycle and collected in any number of pieces. The ledger replaces the Advance / Balance / Payment Status columns of the sheet and keeps the history that the sheet throws away.

Payment entry

  • Fields: member, amount, method (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer from the existing payment methods), date, note, optional "against subscription".
  • Needs an open register, same rule as Customer Due Receive. The row stores counter, outlet, user and time, which is what makes it appear on the register close.
  • Over-payment becomes advance credit and is used by the next cycle automatically.
  • Refund and manual adjustment entries exist (Admin only) with a reason.

Lists that replace the monthly chase

  • Dues: every member with balance above zero, sorted by amount, with phone and last payment date.
  • Expiring: end date within N days, with a Renew button on each row.
  • Expired, not renewed: stopped taking food or still coming? The register shows if they were served after expiry, so the client can decide.

5.9 Register report: one "Mess Collection" card

The register close currently builds one row per payment method with Opening + Sale + Due Receive − Purchase − Due Payment − Expense − Refund. Mess Collection is added as one more positive column in that same formula, and shown as one card on top.

5.11 Settings and roles

Settings (per company)
  • Meal slots: names and time windows (Breakfast 06:30–10:00, Lunch 12:00–15:00, Dinner 19:00–23:00). The register defaults to the slot open now.
  • Default cycle days (30) and expiring alert window (3 days).
  • Skip credit policy: none / credit if informed before slot start / always.
  • Require open register for mess payments (default on).
  • Hide mess dishes from POS grid (default on).
  • Auto-close slot at window end if not closed (posts consumption using planned portions, flagged for review).
  • WhatsApp text templates: daily menu, due reminder, expiry reminder.
Roles (via tbl_access, like every module)
  • Admin: everything, reopen day close, adjustments, settings.
  • Mess Manager: members, subscriptions, menu, sheet, day close, reports.
  • Counter: Meal Register, Extras, Payments, Today.
  • Kitchen: Today, Daily Sheet, Daily Menu, Day Close.
  • Access ids continue from the current max (370): one parent "mess" with add / view / delete / close / settings / report children.

4 language files

Ledger math (no separate ledger table)

member balance = Σ agreed_price          (subscriptions, not voided)
               + Σ extras.total          (settle_type = on_account)
               − Σ payments.amount       (type = payment or advance)
               + Σ payments.amount       (type = refund)
               ± adjustments

Per-subscription "Advance / Balance" (the columns the client is used to) = agreed_price − payments linked to that subscription.

08 Phases

Built in the order the client can use it. Phase 1 already replaces the Excel; each later phase adds a loop. Effort is a rough size for planning, not a quote.

Phase 1 Members and money rough 6–8 dev days

  • Plans, Members, Subscriptions (meals × persons, agreed price, pause, renew, pro-rata).
  • Payments with open-register guard, member ledger, dues list, expiring list.
  • Register close card, register report column, Z report line.
  • One-time import of the current Excel (three sheets) into members and subscriptions.
  • Access rows, sidebar, language keys, migration.

Phase 2 Daily operations rough 6–8 dev days

  • Daily Menu planner with copy and weekly template.
  • Sheet generation rules, Meal Register with bulk marking, advance skips.
  • Extras with pay-now and on-account.
  • Daily Sheet print view and WhatsApp menu text; Today dashboard.
  • Attendance matrix and Daily served summary reports.

Phase 3 Inventory rough 4–6 dev days

  • Stock check on the sheet (need vs current stock, shortage list).
  • Day Close: cooked, leftover, consumption posting, waste creation, reopen.
  • Consumption and variance report, Mess P&L, member statement.
  • Hide mess-only dishes from POS.

Later Nice to have

  • Member QR card scanned at the counter to mark Served.
  • Delivery route list grouped by building, with a driver login.
  • Automatic WhatsApp reminders for dues and expiry (needs a sending provider).
  • Member self-service page: see menu, skip tomorrow, request extras.

Phase 3 depends on Phase 2 (day close needs the menu and served counts). Phase 1 stands alone and can go live first.
